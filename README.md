# Forex Price Prediction Model

## Objective

we aim to build a machine learning model that predicts the future movements or prices of forex currency pairs, such as EUR/USD or GBP/USD. The model will utilize historical and real-time forex data, economic indicators, and potentially sentiment analysis from news sources to forecast price movements and assist with trading strategies.

### Key Outputs:
- **Price Prediction**: Predict the next value (or range of values) for a currency pair
- **Directional Movement**: Predict whether the price will go up, down, or remain stable
- **Volatility Prediction**: Assess the expected level of price fluctuations.

### Use Cases:
- **Trading Signals**: Generate buy/sell signals based on predicted price movements.
- **Risk Management**: Identify volatile periods to adjust trading strategies accordingly.
- **Portfolio Optimization**: Recommend diversification or hedging strategies based on prediction.

### Time Horizon:
- **Short-Term**: Predict movements over minutes or hours, suitable for day trading or scalping.
- **Medium-Term**: Predict movements over days, suitable for swing Trading.
- **Long-Term**: Predict movements over weeks or months, suitable for position trading.
